About Me

My passion for design began in high school, where I started creating digital artwork using Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, and 3D design tools. Throughout my high school years, I consistently explored and refined my creative skills. As I prepared for college, I became interested in understanding how computers work and learning how to code, which led me to pursue a career in UX/UI design and front-end development. I graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and a minor in Mathematics. I am also a member of the National Society of Collegiate Scholars and the National Society of Black Engineers.

Smart Travel Project

This is a project I created in HTML5 and CSS3 with some JavaScript for a form. The idea of this project is that users can easily know and access all the information they need need to travel to the place they want to go for vacation or business. The website is a working idea that guides users to various resources for the destination they want to travel to. These resources can the best food restaurants, the best places, and others.

Employment and Unemployment Project
