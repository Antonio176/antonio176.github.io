Email Marketing Campaign

Designing and optimizing a responsive promotional email campaign

View Project Details

Project Overview

This project involved designing an email campaign to promote a new product launch. The goal was to increase engagement, improve click-through rates, and ensure compatibility across major email clients.

Project Details

  • Role: Email Designer & Developer
  • Tools Used:Photoshop, Figma
  • Email Platform: Campaign automation platform
  • Duration: 2 Days

Design Process

The email was first designed as a wireframe before creating the visual layout. The design focused on mobile responsiveness, accessibility, and clear call-to-action button.

Email Design Preview

Campaign Goals

  • Target Open Rate: 25–35%
  • Expected CTR: 6–10%
  • Goal: Drive traffic to a product landing page and increase product awareness

The campaign demonstrates best practices in email marketing design, including responsive layout, clear content hierarchy, and strategic call-to-action placement to encourage user engagement.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Responsive Email Design
  • Email Campaign Optimization
  • A/B Testing Strategy
  • Email Performance Analytics
